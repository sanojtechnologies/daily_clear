# Privacy Policy for Daily Clear

**Last Updated**: November 10, 2026
**Published by**: Sanoj Technologies

## Summary

Daily Clear is a daily block puzzle: place every piece so the board ends up empty. Your progress is saved on your phone only. There are no accounts, and no servers of ours receive your data.

The game is free. Its income is an occasional ad from Google AdMob, shown between puzzles. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Your game data stays on your device

Daily Clear saves the following on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

- **Daily results**: for each day you played, whether you solved the puzzle and how many undos you used
- **Streaks**: your current streak and your best streak
- **A puzzle in progress**: the pieces you have placed so far and your undo count
- **Practice**: how many practice puzzles you have solved at each level, and which one comes next
- **Settings**: sound and vibration on or off, dark or light theme, and whether you have seen the "How to play" lesson

None of this is uploaded or sent to us.

- **Clearing the game's storage (in your phone's app settings):** erases everything the game stored on this phone.
- **Uninstalling:** removes everything the game stored.
- **Phone backup:** if you use your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account), your phone may restore this progress on a new or reset device. That backup is handled by your phone and Google, not by us.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows an occasional full-screen ad served by Google AdMob, a Google service. The rules it follows:

- An ad can appear only after you have solved a puzzle and tapped to move on, never while you are playing
- No more than one ad every three minutes, and none in the first three minutes after you open the game
- No ad until you have finished more than two puzzles
- Never after you give up, watch a solution or leave a puzzle unfinished

If no ad is ready, for example with no internet connection, the game simply carries on.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following automatically:

- **Device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location
- **App interactions**: for example app launches, taps and ad views
- **Diagnostic information**: for example app launch time and performance

On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving ad services (such as Topics and attribution reporting).

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads need an internet connection. Playing the game never does.

**Consent**: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with **Ad privacy choices** in Settings.

**Your choices**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

## Sharing

When you tap **Share** after a daily puzzle, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short text: the puzzle number, your result, your streak and a picture of the starting board, which is the same for every player. It never includes your moves or the solution. If no share menu is available, the text is copied to your clipboard instead. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: for ads only
- **Advertising ID and ad services**: used by Google's ads SDK, as described above
- **Vibrate**: for the optional tap feedback, which you can switch off in Settings
- **Wake lock and foreground service**: added by Google's ads SDK and its supporting libraries

None of these shows a permission prompt. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or notifications.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control, as described above. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and controls.

For users in the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.
